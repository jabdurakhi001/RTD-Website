About Us

Built for
Owner-Operators

Right Truck Deal LLC was founded in 2017 in Missouri and is headquartered in St. Charles. We provide flexible equipment solutions to support owner-operators and fleets with short-term and long-term needs.

Our mission is straightforward: deliver reliable trucks with transparent terms so you can focus on growing your business, not worrying about your equipment.
